# Launch Runbook

The launch commander confirms all release gates, migrations, backups/restores, rollback, secrets, telemetry, alerts, domains, pilot evidence, support coverage, and formal acceptance. Record go/no-go participants and decision. Route traffic gradually where the provider supports it; monitor readiness, error budgets, queues, retries, provider circuits, authorization anomalies, tenant-isolation indicators, database capacity, notifications, AI usage/cost, and critical incidents.

On breach, stop rollout, pause external work as appropriate, invoke rollback, preserve evidence, and communicate through the incident plan. Expansion is prohibited until stability and separate Phase 20 approval. Launch has not occurred.
