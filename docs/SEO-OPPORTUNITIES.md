# SEO Opportunities and Measurement

Active opportunities are deduplicated by evidence identity. Score version 1 combines bounded search potential, business value, relevance, confidence, urgency, and effort and stores every input. Recommendations state a hypothesis, evidence, risk, effort, approval, execution target, and measurement plan. SEO requests Content or manual work through shared workflows and does not mutate Content records. Completion requires live evidence. Outcomes are positive, neutral, negative, inconclusive, or not measurable and never claim causation from timing alone.
