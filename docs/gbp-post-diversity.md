# GBP post diversity policy

Automated Google Business Profile post generation uses a deterministic source-mix policy before AI drafting.

- Explicitly selected reviews remain authoritative.
- Automated posts begin with business/topic knowledge rather than defaulting to a review.
- Review-derived posts cannot run consecutively.
- The recent generation window is kept at or below a 50% review-derived share whenever business knowledge is available.
- Legacy execution provenance is recognized so existing review-heavy histories immediately influence the next selection.
- Review content is supporting evidence, not a required testimonial-story opening.
- Recent post text remains part of the AI input so openings, angles, structure, and CTA language can be varied.
- Existing approval, media, CTA, source-provenance, and provider-publishing guardrails are unchanged.
