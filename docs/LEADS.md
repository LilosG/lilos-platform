# Leads Data, Consent, and Lifecycle

Lead sources must be verified and active. Every source event has a stable external identity and canonical submission hash. Duplicate delivery is idempotent; probable person/request duplicates are linked without deletion or false identity certainty. Lead contact fields are personal data, excluded from list responses, logs, audit metadata, unrelated analytics, and AI context.

Consent is append-only evidence by channel and purpose. Transactional and marketing permissions are separate; unknown, denied, withdrawn, or expired consent never authorizes communication. Suppression is evaluated immediately before durable send intent and cancels queued follow-up. Received, acknowledged, attempted, delivered, human contact, assignment, and conversion timestamps remain distinct.
