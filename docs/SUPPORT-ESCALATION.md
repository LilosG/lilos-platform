# Support Escalation Plan

Security, tenant isolation, credential exposure, audit-integrity loss, destructive migration, or duplicate external action immediately pages engineering, security, and operations owners and pauses affected capabilities. Provider or product degradation follows the owning runbook and may remain tenant-visible only through sanitized status summaries.

Record reporter, impact, correlation/release, timeline, mitigations, customer communication owner, evidence preservation, and resolution. Never place customer content or secrets in tickets. Names, paging destinations, legal/privacy contacts, and availability rotations remain external sign-off items.
