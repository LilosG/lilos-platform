# Runtime controls

Controls are immutable effective-dated operational restrictions at organization, location, or
registered-product scope. States are allowed, degraded, paused, and disabled. The evaluator selects
the most restrictive applicable state; broad restriction beats narrow enablement, and the result
identifies the winning record, scope, reason, and state.

Mutation requires fixed permission and AAL2. Controls never grant authentication, authorization,
entitlement, readiness, approval, or provider capability and execute no workflow.
