# Reviews Product

Reviews are deduplicated by provider resource and external identity; content hashes distinguish duplicate delivery from provider edits. Original revisions remain immutable and removed reviews retain tombstones. Rating and sentiment are separate. Deterministic risk candidates override low-confidence AI and create restricted escalation cases without making legal conclusions.

Manual drafting always works. AI uses the shared task registry/gateway and approved fact references; outputs remain immutable, attributable, unapproved revisions. Review edits supersede pending/current approvals. Restricted reviews cannot auto-publish. Publication is current-revision, mapping, capability, approval, entitlement, readiness, runtime-control, idempotency, and verification gated; ambiguity reconciles without blind retry.
